RealtorDesk 0.1.17

What's new

- Hidden the internal ID column on the Properties tab.
- Removed Notes from the Properties table. Existing notes remain saved and editable in the property window.
- Added a Closed Price column, displayed as dollars and cents and sorted numerically.
- Enter or update the closed price in the property editor, including when editing linked properties from a client.
- Leave the price blank until recorded, or clear it if needed. Existing property prices start blank.

Client links, property addresses, notes and Key Dates are preserved.
