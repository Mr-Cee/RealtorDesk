RealtorDesk 0.1.31

What's new

- Add/Edit Task now shows a read-only Phone row when the linked client has a phone number.
- The row updates immediately when you add or remove a client. With no client or no saved phone number, the entire row is hidden.
- The popup adjusts to the extra row while keeping the compact layout.
- The main Tasks table is unchanged. Phone numbers are read from the client record rather than copied into tasks.

Existing tasks, reminders, client links, Closed Price values and backups are preserved.
