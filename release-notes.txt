RealtorDesk 0.1.22

What's new

- Replaced the inline client search/dropdown in property information with a linked-client display and Add/Remove button.
- Add opens a popup listing all clients, with instant name, phone and email search at the top.
- Double-click a client, or select a row and click Add at the bottom, to link them.
- Cancel, Escape or closing the popup leaves the assignment unchanged.
- Once linked, the button becomes Remove. Removing clears the pending client link; save the property to commit the change.

Closed Price, MLS numbers, client records and property details are preserved.
