RealtorDesk 0.1.16

What's new

- Delete mistaken property records from the Properties tab. Confirmation names the property and warns if a client is attached. The client itself is kept.

- Property addresses now have separate Street number, Direction, Street name, Street type, Unit/Apt, City, State and ZIP fields.
- Street number, Street name, City, State and ZIP are required when creating or editing a property. Direction, Street type and Unit/Apt are optional.
- Possible duplicate clients are flagged by matching name, email or phone, including common phone formatting differences.
- Possible duplicate properties are flagged by address, including common direction/street-type abbreviations. Units distinguish apartments and condos.
- Review matching records before choosing whether to save anyway. Existing records are not automatically merged or removed.
- Existing full addresses are preserved. The editor prefills recognizable address parts and shows the original address for review; fill missing required details before saving an edited property.
- Linked property details remain synchronized between Clients and Properties. Key Dates are preserved.
