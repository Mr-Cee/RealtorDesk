RealtorDesk 0.1.14

What's new

- Added a Key Dates section below Client information in Add/Edit Client.
- Track Start, Offer, and Close dates independently using the calendar chooser, Today, or Clear.
- All milestones are optional and saved with the client for future timing reports.
- Dates are stored as separate standard date fields. Unknown milestones remain empty and will not be treated as zero-duration transactions in averages.

Existing clients begin with blank Key Dates. Existing client information, communication dates, and linked records are preserved.
