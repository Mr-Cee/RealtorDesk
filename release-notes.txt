RealtorDesk 0.1.25

What's new

- New Reports tab with average Start-to-Offer, Offer-to-Close and Start-to-Close times, plus average sold-home Closed Price.
- Run Overall reports or group by property city and state. Each average shows its sample count.
- All time is the default. Choose a month, quarter, year or custom inclusive date range using calendar choosers.
- Date filtering uses Offer date for Start-to-Offer and Close date for the other metrics. Closing dates come from client Key Dates.
- Timings count clients once overall, or once per city. Price averages count properties marked Sold or Closed with a recorded Closed Price.
- Missing or reversed timing dates are excluded. Sold properties without a linked Close date are included only in All time. A dash indicates no qualifying data.

Existing records, documents and your chosen data folder are preserved.
