What's new

- Click a date field directly to open its calendar. Separate Choose date buttons have been removed.
- Applies to report date ranges, Date Last Communicated and client Key Dates.
- Keyboard users can focus the date field and press Enter, Space or Alt+Down to open the calendar.
- Clear remains available. Disabled report date fields stay inactive until Custom range is selected.

Existing dates, records, documents and Closed Price values are preserved.
