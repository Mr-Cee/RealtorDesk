RealtorDesk 0.1.29

What's new

Backups and restore
- Open Settings > Backups / Restore to create a backup, restore one, or open the backup folder.
- Full backups include a consistent database snapshot, documents and saved files, with checksum verification.
- Automatic backups run every 10 minutes while the app is open when data has changed. Keep the latest five automatic copies; manual backups are not trimmed.
- Restore verifies a backup and switches to a separate empty folder. Current data and a safety backup are retained. The app reloads and remembers the restored location.

Tasks and reminders
- New Tasks tab with due dates, priorities, notes, completion tracking, and optional client/property links.
- Search tasks instantly; filter to due/overdue tasks or include completed tasks.
- Optional in-app reminders show due and overdue tasks once per day while RealtorDesk is open, including on startup. The app must be open for reminders.
- Tasks are included in backups and restore.

Existing client links, MLS numbers, Closed Price values, commissions and documents are preserved.
