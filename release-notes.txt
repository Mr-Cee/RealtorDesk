RealtorDesk 0.1.32

What's new

- Select a property and click Checklist to track transaction steps and completed/total progress.
- Start with Buyer or Seller templates, or create your own. Template changes affect future checklists only.
- Add, edit, complete, reopen or delete steps, with optional calendar due dates and notes.
- Steps also appear in Tasks. Completion, edits and due dates stay synchronized, and due steps use existing reminders while the app is open.
- Template steps start without due dates. Add dates to receive reminders.

Existing tasks, reminders, client links, Closed Price values and backups are preserved. Checklists are included in database backups.
